ShibaX1 India Geodata

India's openly-licensed geospatial data — administrative boundaries, census geometries, electoral maps, water and infrastructure networks — collected in one place and ready to download.

Anyone who has worked with Indian geospatial data knows the tax: weeks lost hunting boundary files across scattered government portals, then more weeks reconciling them. This site exists so that work only has to happen once. Researcher, policy analyst, student, or someone building something interesting — take what you need.

Credit where it's due

The underlying compilation was created by Yashveer Singh and released under CC BY 4.0. This site is a fork of india-geodata, maintained by ShibaX1. Dataset downloads are served from the original repository's release assets.

India Map Maker

What is India Map Maker?

A free online map maker for India, built on the boundaries in this collection. Paste or upload a spreadsheet and it colours a map of India by state, district, sub-district, block, parliamentary constituency or assembly constituency. It runs in your browser and nothing is uploaded. Open the map maker.

Read moreRead less

What does it cost?

Nothing. There is no sign-up, no watermark and no paid tier. The code is open source and the boundaries are open government data.

What data can I map?

Any table with a name column and a number column: literacy rates, population, election results, sales by district. Misspelt names are flagged so you can fix them on the spot, or download the template CSV, fill in the value column and upload it back.

Can I map a single state or district?

Yes. Choose all of India, one state or one district, then divide it by any level: 36 states and union territories, 785 districts, sub-districts, blocks, 543 parliamentary constituencies or 4,177 assembly constituencies.

What can I export?

PNG at two sizes, SVG for editing in Illustrator or Inkscape, PDF for print, the data as CSV, and a project file you can reopen later.
